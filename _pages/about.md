Hello there! I am a recent Mathematics MS graduate from [NYU Courant](https://math.nyu.edu/dynamic/) currently doing research with Profs. [Lerrel Pinto](https://www.lerrelpinto.com/), [Amine Aboussalah](https://quantumgeometricintelligence.com/people/amine-mohamed-aboussalah), and most recently [Ben Riviere](https://sites.google.com/view/riviere-robot-lab/home?authuser=0).

I am broadly interested in world models, reinforcement learning, and optimization. My primary motivation is to develop robots that can plan and explore in space. 

I previously completed my BS at NYU as well, working with Profs. [Esteban Tabak](https://cims.nyu.edu/people/profiles/TABAK_Esteban.html) at Courant and [Debra Laefer](https://engineering.nyu.edu/faculty/debra-laefer) at the NYU Center for Urban Science and Progress.
