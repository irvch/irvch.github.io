Hello there! 

I am a recent Mathematics MS graduate from [NYU Courant](https://math.nyu.edu/dynamic/) currently doing research at [NYU CILVR](https://wp.nyu.edu/cilvr/), where I have been working under [Kathy Zhou](https://gaoyuezhou.github.io/) and Prof. [Lerrel Pinto](https://www.lerrelpinto.com/). My work broadly focuses on latent-space world models, reinforcement learning, and optimization. My primary motivation is to develop robots that can plan over long-horizons and explore difficult environments like space. 

This website is still a work in progress. Lots of things to do! Notes from courses I've taken and TA'd will be added soon. Perhaps I'll feel brave enough to share about my hobbies - art, cooking, watching movies, reading comics, etc.

I previously received my BS at NYU as well, working on optimal transport for remote sensing and perception with Profs. [Esteban Tabak](https://cims.nyu.edu/people/profiles/TABAK_Esteban.html) at Courant and [Debra Laefer](https://engineering.nyu.edu/faculty/debra-laefer) at the NYU Center for Urban Science and Progress.
