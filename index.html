Hello there! I am a recent Mathematics MS graduate from NYU Courant currently doing research at NYU CILVR, where I have been working under Kathy Zhou and Prof. Lerrel Pinto. My work broadly focuses on latent-space world models, reinforcement learning, and optimization. My primary motivation is to develop robots that can plan over long-horizons and explore difficult environments like space. This website is still a work in progress. Lots of things to do!

I previously received my BS at NYU as well, working with Profs. Esteban Tabak at Courant and Debra Laefer at the NYU Center for Urban Science and Progress.
